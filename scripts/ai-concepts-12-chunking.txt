DAD: Why would an AI system split my big PDF into smaller pieces?
TEEN: That's called chunking. A document is divided into smaller sections so each part can be processed and searched. | ✂️ Chunking ; smaller sections
DAD: Why not keep the whole document together?
TEEN: Smaller chunks can make retrieval more focused and help fit information into a model's context window.
DAD: Can chunks be too small?
TEEN: Yes. They might lose useful context. Very large chunks can make search less precise.
