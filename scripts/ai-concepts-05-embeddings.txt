DAD: How can a computer find two sentences with similar meanings?
TEEN: AI systems can use embeddings. They turn information, like words or images, into lists of numbers called vectors. | 🔢 Vectors ; numerical representation
DAD: But how can numbers represent meaning?
TEEN: A model learns to place related items near each other in a mathematical space.
DAD: So puppy and dog could be close together?
TEEN: Exactly! That helps systems find related content even when the wording is different.
