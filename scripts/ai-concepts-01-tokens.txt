DAD: Does ChatGPT read every word exactly the way we do?
TEEN: Not quite! Before a language model processes text, it breaks it into smaller pieces called tokens. | 🧩 Tokens ; pieces of text
DAD: Are tokens always complete words?
TEEN: No. A token can be a whole word, part of a word, punctuation, or another text unit.
DAD: So one sentence can become lots of tokens?
TEEN: Exactly! The model processes those tokens as numerical IDs. Token counts affect how much text fits in a request and can affect usage costs.
DAD: So word count and token count aren't the same?
