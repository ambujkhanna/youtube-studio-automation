DAD: Why does AI sometimes give a confident answer that's completely wrong?
TEEN: That can be an AI hallucination: information that sounds convincing but is incorrect or made up. | ⚠️ Hallucination ; plausible but wrong
DAD: Why does that happen?
TEEN: A language model generates likely text. That doesn't guarantee every statement is factually correct.
DAD: Should we stop using AI?
TEEN: No! Use it thoughtfully. Check important facts, ask for sources and verify those sources independently.
