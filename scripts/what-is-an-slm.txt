TEEN: Dad, I know about ChatGPT. But what's this new thing called SLM?
DAD: SLM stands for Small Language Model. It's a compact version of an AI language model!
TEEN: Small? Like a mini ChatGPT?
DAD: That's a good way to imagine it! SLMs are designed to use fewer computing resources while handling specific tasks efficiently.   | 🧠 Compact AI ; fewer resources
TEEN: What kind of tasks?
DAD: Summarising notes, answering simple questions, generating smart replies, and even helping with coding!
TEEN: Wait! Do I need the internet to use one?
DAD: Not always! Some SLMs can run directly on your laptop or smartphone, even offline.   | 💻 Runs locally ; sometimes offline
TEEN: So my laptop can become an AI assistant without connecting to the cloud?
DAD: Exactly! That's one of their biggest advantages. Your device can process certain AI tasks locally.
TEEN: Then why isn't everyone using SLMs?
DAD: Because smaller models may struggle with some complex tasks that larger models handle better. Choosing the right model depends on what you need.
