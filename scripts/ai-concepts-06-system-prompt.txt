DAD: Why can an AI assistant act like a tutor or a coding helper?
TEEN: Its behaviour can be guided by system instructions, often called a system prompt. | 📋 Instructions ; role and rules
DAD: What might those instructions say?
TEEN: They could say, "Explain maths in simple language," or "Answer like a helpful coding assistant."
DAD: So they guide how the AI responds?
TEEN: Yes. They can define a role, style, boundaries and other rules.
DAD: Does that guarantee the AI always follows them?
TEEN: No. Instructions guide behaviour, but systems still need testing and safeguards.
