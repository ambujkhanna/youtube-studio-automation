DAD: Did you know you can run an AI chatbot directly on your own computer?
TEEN: Yes! It's possible with Ollama.   | 🤖 AI on your PC
DAD: Ollama? What exactly is it?
TEEN: Ollama lets you download and run AI models like Llama locally, instead of sending every question to an online AI service.   | 💻 Local AI
DAD: But why would someone do that?
TEEN: More privacy, control over your models, and no cloud API charges. Your computer does the processing!
DAD: Sounds interesting! How do we get started?
TEEN: Just visit ollama.com, then download and install Ollama for your operating system.   | 🌐 Ollama website
DAD: And how do we run our first AI?
TEEN: Open your terminal and type: ollama run llama3.2. Ollama downloads the model if needed, then you can start chatting!   | ⌨️ ollama run llama3.2
DAD: So, our computer becomes an AI assistant?
TEEN: Exactly! Just remember, performance depends on your computer's hardware.
