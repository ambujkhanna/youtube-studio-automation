DAD: Can an AI read an unlimited amount of information at once?
TEEN: No! Language models have a limit called a context window. Think of it like a desk: only a certain amount of paperwork fits at one time. | 🪟 Context ; working space
DAD: What goes on that desk?
TEEN: Your latest message, relevant conversation history, instructions and sometimes retrieved documents.
DAD: What if the conversation gets too long?
TEEN: Older details may be left out or summarized, depending on the application.
DAD: So a bigger context window fits more information?
TEEN: Yes, but more space doesn't guarantee perfect understanding.
