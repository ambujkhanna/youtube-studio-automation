**Status:** Working channel identity; positioning and brand checks pending.

## Channel role

Channel 1 in the YouTube Studio Automation project. This is the first channel to be developed and the proving ground for the shared Shorts production engine.

## Working positioning

Practical AI and technology demonstrations, experiments, workflows, and clear explanations for a global English-speaking audience.

The name **AImation Studio** may suggest AI-generated animation. Do not assume that the channel is limited to animation until the owner confirms the intended positioning. If animation is part of the content mix, use it as a visual storytelling technique while retaining the agreed AI/technology focus unless the editorial brief changes.

## Initial publishing target

- Format: YouTube Shorts
- Language: English
- Audience: Global
- Initial duration target: approximately 30–60 seconds
- Initial publishing time: daily at 7:00 PM IST, only after the first complete pilot passes QC and the connected YouTube destination is verified.

## Editorial principles

- Demonstrate or explain one useful idea per Short.
- Prefer real workflows and reproducible demonstrations over generic AI claims.
- Source-check factual claims and distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Avoid fabricated tool capabilities, screenshots, benchmarks, quotations, and results.
- Use original or properly licensed visuals, voice, music, and other assets.
- Keep on-screen text readable on a phone and captions accurate.

## Launch gates

1. Confirm the channel's exact editorial promise and visual identity.
2. Check YouTube channel name/handle availability and relevant trademark conflicts.
3. Verify that the connected Metricool YouTube destination is this channel.
4. Produce and review the first complete Short.
5. Enable unattended daily publishing only after the production and fail-safe checks work reliably.
