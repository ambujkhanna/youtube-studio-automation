## How it runs
- **Daily Short** (`.github/workflows/daily-short.yml`, 09:15 UTC = 14:45 IST): the next ready `episodes/epNNN-*/episode.json` goes through voices (Kokoro: DAD am_michael, TEEN af_heart), frames (skia: living room `BG=v2`, big whiteboard 40/150/1000/700, doodle characters, shading, colour grade), captions, a per-episode original music track, QC (1080×1920, 45–180 s), and upload in the PUBLISH_WINDOW (default 17:00–20:00 IST). Then the 3D-style thumbnail is set (the channel is verified), `episodes/state.json` is updated, and the MP4 is kept as an artifact.
- **Weekly long video** (`.github/workflows/weekly-long.yml`, Sunday 04:30 UTC): `pipeline/daily.py --long` takes the next `long/LNN-*/episode.json` and renders a 16:9 1920×1080 video of 3–8 minutes (board left, characters right, string lights). Chapters come from `"chapter"` markers, and there's a 16:9 thumbnail and no #Shorts. State is kept in `long/state.json`.
- **Key files:**
  - `pipeline/episode_engine.py`: scenes, including chat, permission, title, big, steps, compare, checklist, summary, gallery and poem; diagrams via `diagrams.py`; acting and camera.
  - `kit2d.py`: frame composition, fonts (Poppins), the 16:9 layout, grade.
  - `char_line.py`, `bg_line.py`: drawing the characters and the room.
  - `cta.py`: the Subscribe / Like / Share end card and the mid-video reminder.
  - `music.py`, `thumbnail.py`, `images.py` (Bioicons `img:` drawings and auto credits), `validate_episode.py`.
- **Content:** EP001 was a manual pilot. EP002–EP038 are Shorts; EP002–EP006 are uploaded (see state.json). Long scripts L01–L08 are ready (How ChatGPT works, Why AI makes things up, Spot a deepfake, What not to tell AI, How AI learns, AI scams, How AI makes pictures, Smart everyday AI).
